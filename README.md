## 📲 How to Install on Your Android Phone

Follow these 4 simple steps to install the APK:

```text
  [1] Tap Download Link    ➔    [2] Open Downloaded APK    ➔    [3] Allow Installation    ➔    [4] Sign In & Enjoy!
 (Notification / Browser)         (Tap on .apk file)             (Toggle from settings)           (House No + PIN)
```

1. **Download:** Tap the [Download APK](https://github.com/knarsimharao/colony-fund-app/releases/download/v1.7.0/ColonyFund-v1.7.0-Build47.apk) link on your Android smartphone.
2. **Open:** Once downloaded, swipe down from the top of your screen and tap the downloaded file notification (or open your phone's **Files / Downloads** app and tap `ColonyFund-v1.7.0-Build47.apk`).
3. **Allow Installation:** If Android shows a prompt saying:  
   > *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*  
   - Tap **Settings**.
   - Turn **ON** the toggle switch next to **Allow from this source** (Chrome / Files).
   - Tap the Back button.
4. **Install & Launch:** Tap **Install**, then tap **Open**.

### 🔑 Signing In:
- Select your House / Flat Number or enter your Registered Mobile Number.
- Enter your 4-digit PIN (default: `1234`).
- Optionally enable **Fingerprint / Face Unlock** for 1-tap biometric access next time!

## ✨ Key Features & Highlights

### 🏛️ Dual-Fund Society Architecture
Colony Fund intelligently separates welfare savings from physical maintenance operations:
1. **Welfare Mutual Fund (₹500/mo):**
   - Personal invested capital & portfolio dividend tracking.
   - Low-interest emergency loans (1.5% p.m.) with flexible repayment amortization.
   - Transparent circulating loan status with borrower counters.
2. **Colony Maintenance Fund (₹100/mo):**
   - Physical cash box management for colony upkeep.
   - Live transparency ledger for streetlights, sanitation, borewell motor repairs, and security bills.
   - Receipts collected vs. total spent breakdown.

---

### 🎨 Fintech-Grade Experience
- **🌙 Global Dark Mode:** Midnight Slate canvas (`#0b0f17`) with glowing linear gradients and soft card elevation. Toggle anytime with 1 tap in the top app bar!
- **🌐 100% Bilingual Support:** Instant toggle between **English** and **తెలుగు (Telugu)** across every dashboard, receipt, and notification.
- **⚡ 1-Tap UPI Clearing:** Pay monthly dues seamlessly using your favorite installed banking app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED).
- **📄 Instant PDF Receipts & Passbooks:** Generate and download official digitally signed PDF receipts and society passbooks directly to your phone.
- **💬 WhatsApp Accounts Integration:** Pre-formatted society statements, dues reminders, and payment receipts with one click to WhatsApp community groups.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

Your community's financial data belongs to your community:
- **Zero Third-Party Tracking:** No ad networks, no analytics brokers, and no hidden telemetry.
- **Private Google Sheets Backend:** All records reside inside your society's private Google account.
- **On-Device Biometrics:** Fingerprint and Face Unlock authentication are handled strictly on your hardware via Android `BiometricPrompt`.
- Read our full [**Privacy Policy**](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
